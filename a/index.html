Hi, I'm Raquel - a neuroscience researcher who loves the hands-on side of molecular biology and cell culture. I'm finishing my M.Sc. in Medical Neuroscience at Charité in Berlin, where I design and run optogenetics experiments in the Dietmar Schmitz Lab.

I'm currently in the process of relocating to the San Francisco Bay Area to join the Kaufer Lab as visiting student researcher at UC Berkeley, studying stress vulnerability and resilience in mouse models. Across six lab rotations I've worked on molecular cloning, PCR, CRISPR/Cas9 gene editing, and confocal imaging - I keep meticulous, reproducible records, troubleshoot experiments on my own, and pick up new techniques quickly at the bench.
